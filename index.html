Westward

By Jason Heppler. An endless scroll through the American West in public-domain photographs from open cultural heritage collections.

Photographs come from the Library of Congress, the Portal to Texas History at the University of North Texas, and the DeGolyer Library at Southern Methodist University, partners across the Digital Public Library of America, and the Smithsonian Every image is public domain, is served by its holding institution, and links back to its source record.

Colophon

Westward is a static site built on a small pipeline that queries each institution's open API and writes flat JSON. The photographs themselves are never rehosted. Set in Georgia. Source on GitHub. Built in collaboration with Claude.
